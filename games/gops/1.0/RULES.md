# GOPS (Game Of Pure Strategy)

## Overview
GOPS is a bidding game where players simultaneously bid cards to win prize cards. Despite its name, it involves both strategy and psychology.

## Players
2 players (variants exist for 3-4)

## Equipment
- One standard 52-card deck

## Objective
Win the most valuable prize cards (by total value).

## Setup
1. Separate deck by suits:
   - One suit becomes prize cards (traditionally Diamonds)
   - Each player gets one complete suit as bidding cards (13 cards)
   - Remaining suit is not used
2. Shuffle prize cards and place face down

## Play
1. Reveal top prize card
2. Each player secretly selects a bid card from their hand
3. Simultaneously reveal bid cards
4. Highest bid wins the prize card
5. In case of tie, the prize carries over to next round
6. Used bid cards are discarded (each card used only once)
7. Continue until all 13 prize cards are claimed

## Card Values
- Ace = 1 point
- Number cards = Face value (2-10)
- Jack = 11 points
- Queen = 12 points
- King = 13 points

## Scoring
Add up the values of all prize cards won. Total of 91 points available.
Winner is player with score over 45 (or highest if tied at 45).

## Variations
- **Random Order**: Shuffle prize cards (standard)
- **Ascending Order**: Reveal prizes from Ace to King
- **Player Choice**: Winner of each round chooses next prize
- **Three Players**: Use three suits for bidding, one for prizes
- **Blind GOPS**: Don't reveal prize until after bidding

## Strategy
- Each bid card can only be used once - timing is crucial
- Consider saving high cards for high-value prizes
- Try to win with minimal overbidding
- Bluff by occasionally bidding high on low prizes
- In ties, consider if the carryover makes next round more valuable
