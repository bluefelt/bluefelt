# Hearts

## Overview
Hearts is a trick-avoidance game where players try to avoid winning certain cards that carry penalty points.

## Players
4 players (variants exist for 3-6)

## Equipment
- One standard 52-card deck

## Objective
Have the lowest score when someone reaches 100 points.

## Setup
- Deal 13 cards to each player
- Players arrange cards by suit

## Passing
Before each hand (except sometimes the 4th):
1. Choose 3 cards to pass:
   - 1st hand: Pass left
   - 2nd hand: Pass right  
   - 3rd hand: Pass across
   - 4th hand: No passing (optional)
2. Must choose cards before receiving passed cards

## Play
1. Player with 2♣ leads first trick (must play 2♣)
2. Players must follow suit if possible
3. If unable to follow suit, may play any card
4. No points cards (hearts or Q♠) on first trick unless unavoidable
5. Highest card of led suit wins trick
6. Trick winner leads next trick

## Restrictions
- Cannot lead hearts until hearts are "broken" (someone plays a heart when unable to follow suit)
- Exception: If hearts are all you have left

## Scoring
At end of each hand:
- Each heart: 1 point
- Queen of Spades: 13 points
- Total: 26 points per hand

## Shooting the Moon
If one player takes ALL penalty cards (all 13 hearts + Q♠):
- Option 1: Give 26 points to each opponent
- Option 2: Subtract 26 points from your score

## Game End
Play until someone reaches 100 points. Lowest score wins.

## Variations
- **Jack of Diamonds**: -10 points (bonus)
- **Omnibus Hearts**: 10♣ worth -10 points
- **Spot Hearts**: Hearts worth their face value (face cards 10 each)
- **Partnership Hearts**: Partners sit opposite, combine scores

## Strategy
- Count cards to track dangerous suits
- Try to void a suit early (preferably spades)
- High spades are dangerous before Q♠ is played
- Low hearts are often safe to keep
- Watch for shooting attempts and stop them
