# War

## Overview
War is a simple card game of chance where players simultaneously reveal cards and the highest card wins.

## Players
2 players (variants exist for more)

## Equipment
- One standard 52-card deck

## Objective
Win all 52 cards.

## Setup
1. Shuffle deck thoroughly
2. Deal entire deck evenly between players (26 cards each)
3. Players keep their cards face down in a pile

## Card Rankings
From highest to lowest: A, K, Q, J, 10, 9, 8, 7, 6, 5, 4, 3, 2

## Play
1. Each player simultaneously turns over their top card
2. Player with the higher card wins both cards
3. Winner places both cards face down at bottom of their pile
4. If cards are equal rank, WAR begins

## War (Tie)
When players reveal cards of equal rank:
1. Each player places 3 cards face down (or 1 if traditional)
2. Each player reveals one more card face up
3. Higher card wins all cards from that war
4. If still tied, repeat war process
5. If a player runs out of cards during war, they use their last card

## Winning
The game ends when one player has all 52 cards.

## Variations
- **Shorter Games**: Play for a set time limit or until one player has a certain number of cards
- **Three Card War**: Place 3 cards face down during war (instead of 1)
- **Automatic War**: Certain cards (like Jokers) automatically trigger war
- **Multiple Players**: With 3+ players, highest card wins all

## Notes
- This is purely a game of chance with no decisions
- Games can last very long or end quickly
- Some players shuffle won cards before adding to their pile
- Card counting is possible but rarely done due to the game's random nature
