# Cheat (I Doubt It)

## Overview
Cheat is a shedding game where players must be deceptive about the cards they play. Also known as "I Doubt It," "Bluff," or "BS."

## Players
3-10 players (best with 4-6)

## Equipment
- One standard 52-card deck (two decks for 6+ players)

## Objective
Be the first to get rid of all your cards.

## Setup
- Deal entire deck as evenly as possible among all players
- Players may look at their cards
- Choose a starting player

## Play
1. First player places 1-4 cards face down and declares "One Ace" (or "Two Aces," etc.)
2. Next player must claim to play 2s, then 3s, continuing through K, then back to A
3. Players must claim the required rank but may lie about what they actually play
4. Any player may challenge by saying "Cheat!" (or "I doubt it!")

## Challenging
When someone calls "Cheat!":
1. Reveal the played cards
2. If the player lied (cards don't match claim):
   - They take the entire discard pile
3. If the player told the truth:
   - The challenger takes the entire discard pile
4. The player who picks up cards plays next

## Rules
- Must play at least 1 card
- Maximum 4 cards per turn (or number of that rank in deck)
- Must claim the required rank in sequence
- Can play cards face down in any way (stack, spread, etc.)

## Winning
First player to get rid of all cards wins.
If challenged on final cards and telling truth, they win immediately.

## Variations
- **Allow Jokers**: Jokers are wild and can represent any card
- **Skip Ranks**: Allow playing the previous or next rank
- **Reverse Order**: Count down from K to A
- **Multiple Decks**: Use 2 decks for larger groups

## Strategy
- Keep track of played cards to spot impossible claims
- Bluff early when the pile is small
- Tell the truth when the pile is large
- Challenge when someone claims 4 of a rank you hold 3 of
- Get rid of cards you'll need to lie about later
