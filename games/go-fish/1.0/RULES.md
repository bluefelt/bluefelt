# Go Fish Rules

Go Fish is a classic card collection game where players try to collect pairs (sets of 4 cards of the same rank) by asking other players for cards.

## Setup

- **Players**: 2-4 players
- **Cards**: Standard 52-card deck
- **Deal**: 
  - 2-3 players: 7 cards each
  - 4 players: 5 cards each
- **Remaining cards**: Form a "pool" (fishing pond) face down

## Objective

Collect the most pairs (sets of 4 cards of the same rank). When you get 4 cards of the same rank, they automatically form a pair and are placed in your scoring area.

## Gameplay

### Turn Structure

1. **Ask Phase**: On your turn, choose another player and ask them for all cards of a specific rank you have in your hand
   - You must have at least one card of the rank you're asking for
   - Example: "Sarah, do you have any 7s?"

2. **Response Phase**: The asked player responds:
   - If they have cards of that rank: They give you ALL cards of that rank
   - If they don't have any: They say "Go Fish!"

3. **Draw Phase** (if you went fishing):
   - If the player said "Go Fish!", draw one card from the pool
   - If you draw the rank you asked for, you get another turn
   - Otherwise, your turn ends

4. **Pair Formation**: 
   - Whenever you collect 4 cards of the same rank, they automatically form a pair
   - Pairs are immediately moved to your scoring area and remain visible to all players

5. **Turn Continuation**:
   - If you received cards from another player, you get another turn
   - If you drew the requested rank from the pool, you get another turn
   - Otherwise, play passes to the next player

### Special Rules

- **Must Ask for Owned Rank**: You can only ask for ranks you currently have in your hand
- **All or Nothing**: When a player has the requested rank, they must give you ALL cards of that rank
- **Automatic Pairs**: As soon as you have 4 cards of the same rank, they automatically become a pair
- **Empty Hand**: If your hand becomes empty, immediately draw a card from the pool to continue playing

## Game End

The game ends when either:
1. **Pool Empty**: No more cards remain in the pool to draw
2. **All Pairs Formed**: All 13 possible pairs have been collected

## Scoring

The player with the most pairs wins. In case of a tie, the game is a draw.

## Strategy Tips

- **Memory**: Remember what ranks other players have asked for
- **Timing**: Ask for ranks where you suspect other players have multiple cards
- **Hand Management**: Keep track of which ranks you have multiples of
- **Observation**: Watch which players are collecting which pairs

## Example Turn

1. Player 1 has cards: 3♠, 3♥, 7♦, K♣, A♠
2. Player 1 asks Player 2: "Do you have any 3s?"
3. Player 2 has 3♦ and 3♣, so gives both to Player 1
4. Player 1 now has 3♠, 3♥, 3♦, 3♣ - all four 3s!
5. The four 3s automatically form a pair and move to Player 1's scoring area
6. Player 1's hand now contains: 7♦, K♣, A♠
7. Since Player 1 received cards, they get another turn
