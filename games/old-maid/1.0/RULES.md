# Old Maid

## Overview
Old Maid is a simple matching game where players try to form pairs and avoid being left with the unmatchable Old Maid card.

## Players
2-8 players (best with 3-6)

## Equipment
- Standard 52-card deck with one Queen removed (leaving 51 cards)
- The remaining Queen becomes the "Old Maid"

## Objective
Form pairs of cards by rank and avoid being the player left with the Old Maid.

## Setup
1. Remove one Queen from the deck (typically Queen of Clubs)
2. Deal all 51 cards as evenly as possible among players
3. Players look at their cards and remove any pairs (two cards of same rank)
4. Discarded pairs are placed face up

## Play
1. Player to dealer's left starts
2. On your turn:
   - Fan your cards with backs toward the player on your left
   - That player draws one card from your hand
   - If the drawn card makes a pair with a card in their hand, they discard the pair
   - Turn passes to the left

3. Continue until all possible pairs are made

## Winning
- Players who run out of cards are safe and out of the game
- The player left holding the Old Maid (unpaired Queen) loses

## Variations
- **Commercial Sets**: Use special Old Maid decks with illustrated cards
- **Multiple Losers**: Remove multiple cards to have multiple "old maids"
- **Joker Version**: Add a Joker as the Old Maid instead of removing a Queen

## Strategy
- Try to spread your cards evenly when offering
- Watch which cards others are drawing to guess what they need
- Try to get rid of Queens early if possible
