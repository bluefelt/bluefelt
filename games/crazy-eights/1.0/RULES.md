# Crazy Eights

## Overview
Crazy Eights is a shedding-type card game where players race to get rid of all their cards by matching ranks or suits.

## Players
2-7 players (best with 3-5)

## Equipment
- One standard 52-card deck (two decks for 5+ players)

## Objective
Be the first player to get rid of all your cards.

## Setup
- Deal 5 cards to each player (7 cards if only 2 players)
- Place remaining deck face down as draw pile
- Turn top card face up to start discard pile
- If an 8 is turned up, bury it and turn up next card

## Play
1. Players take turns clockwise
2. On your turn, play a card that matches either:
   - The rank of the top discard
   - The suit of the top discard
   - Any 8 (wild card)
3. If you cannot play, draw cards until you can play or the draw pile is empty
4. After playing an 8, declare the suit for the next player

## Special Cards
- **Eights**: Wild cards that can be played anytime. Player declares new suit.

## Winning
First player to play all their cards wins the round.

## Scoring (Optional)
Winner scores points for cards left in opponents' hands:
- Eights: 50 points
- Face cards (J, Q, K): 10 points
- Aces: 1 point
- Number cards: Face value

First to reach 100 points wins the game.

## Variations
Common house rules may include:
- Draw only one card if unable to play
- Special effects for other cards (2s force next player to draw 2, etc.)
- Must say "last card" when down to one card

## Strategy
- Save eights for when you're stuck or to change to a favorable suit
- Try to empty one suit from your hand early
- Pay attention to what suits opponents are drawing for
