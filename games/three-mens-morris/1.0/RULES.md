# Three Men's Morris Rules

## Overview
Three Men's Morris is an ancient abstract strategy game where two players compete to form a "mill" (three pieces in a row) on a 3x3 grid. Unlike tic-tac-toe, pieces can move after placement, creating a dynamic and engaging game.

## Players
- Minimum: 2
- Maximum: 2

## Equipment
- 3x3 grid board with 9 intersection points
- 3 pieces per player (traditionally different colors)
- Lines connecting adjacent points (horizontal, vertical, and diagonal)

## Setup
- Empty board at start
- Each player has 3 pieces in their supply
- Players alternate turns, starting with Player 1

## Gameplay
### Turn Structure
The game has two phases:

1. **Placement Phase** (First 6 turns)
   - Players take turns placing one piece from their supply onto any empty intersection
   - If a player forms a mill (3 in a row) during placement, they win immediately

2. **Movement Phase** (After all pieces are placed)
   - Players take turns moving one of their pieces
   - A piece can move to any empty intersection on the board
   - Goal is to form a mill (3 in a row)

### Allowed Actions
- **Place**: During placement phase, put a piece from supply onto any empty intersection
- **Move**: During movement phase, move one of your pieces to any empty intersection

## Win Conditions
- Form a mill (3 pieces in a row) horizontally, vertically, or diagonally
- The game cannot end in a draw if played optimally

## Special Rules
- Pieces can move to ANY empty intersection, not just adjacent ones
- Once placed, pieces cannot be removed from the board (only moved)
- A mill can be formed during either placement or movement phase

## Quick Start
Place your three pieces on the board, then move them around trying to get three in a row. First player to line up all three pieces wins!

## Strategy Notes
- The center position (5) is the most valuable as it participates in 4 possible mills
- Corner positions (1,3,7,9) each participate in 3 possible mills
- Edge positions (2,4,6,8) each participate in 2 possible mills
- With optimal play, the first player can force a win
