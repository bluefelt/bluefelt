# Contract Rummy

## Overview
Contract Rummy is a multi-round card game where players must complete specific contracts (melds) in each round. The game uses two standard decks plus jokers.

## Players
3-5 players

## Equipment
- Two standard 52-card decks plus 4 jokers (108 cards total)

## Objective
Complete the required contract for each round and minimize points from remaining cards.

## Setup
- Shuffle both decks together with jokers
- Deal cards based on the round:
  - Rounds 1-3: 10 cards each
  - Rounds 4-7: 12 cards each
- Place remaining deck face down as draw pile
- Turn top card face up to start discard pile

## Rounds and Contracts
1. Two sets of 3
2. One set of 3 and one sequence of 4
3. Two sequences of 4
4. Three sets of 3
5. Two sets of 3 and one sequence of 4
6. One set of 3 and two sequences of 4
7. Three sequences of 4

## Definitions
- **Set**: 3 or more cards of the same rank (e.g., 7♠ 7♥ 7♦)
- **Sequence**: 3 or more consecutive cards of the same suit (e.g., 5♣ 6♣ 7♣)
- **Joker**: Wild card that can represent any card

## Play
1. On your turn:
   - Draw one card from draw pile or discard pile
   - If you can complete your contract, lay down the required melds
   - Discard one card to the discard pile
   
2. After completing your contract:
   - You may lay off cards on any player's melds
   - You may add to your own melds
   
3. The round ends when one player runs out of cards

## Scoring
Cards remaining in hand:
- Number cards: Face value (2-10)
- Face cards (J, Q, K): 10 points each
- Aces: 15 points each
- Jokers: 25 points each

## Winning
After all 7 rounds, the player with the lowest total score wins.

## Special Rules
- Jokers are wild and can represent any card
- Aces can be high or low in sequences (A-2-3 or Q-K-A)
- You cannot pick up the entire discard pile
- You must complete your contract before laying off cards
