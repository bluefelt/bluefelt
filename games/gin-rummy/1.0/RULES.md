# Gin Rummy

## Overview
Gin Rummy is a two-player card game where players form melds and try to minimize deadwood (unmatched cards).

## Players
2 players

## Equipment
- One standard 52-card deck

## Objective
Be the first to reach 100 points by forming melds and minimizing deadwood.

## Setup
- Deal 10 cards to each player
- Place remaining deck face down as draw pile
- Turn top card face up to start discard pile

## Definitions
- **Meld**: Either a set or a run
- **Set**: 3 or 4 cards of the same rank (e.g., 7♠ 7♥ 7♦)
- **Run**: 3 or more consecutive cards of the same suit (e.g., 5♣ 6♣ 7♣)
- **Deadwood**: Cards not in any meld
- **Deadwood Value**: Total point value of deadwood cards

## Card Values
- Aces: 1 point
- Number cards: Face value (2-10)
- Face cards (J, Q, K): 10 points each

## Play
1. On your turn:
   - Draw one card from draw pile or discard pile
   - Discard one card face up
   
2. Continue until someone knocks or goes gin

## Ending a Hand
Three ways to end:

### Going Gin
- Form melds with all 10 cards (no deadwood)
- Lay down all cards
- Score: 25 points + opponent's deadwood

### Knocking
- When deadwood totals 10 points or less
- Lay down melds and deadwood separately
- Opponent may lay off deadwood on your melds
- Score: Difference in deadwood values

### Big Gin
- Draw 11th card and form melds with all 11 cards
- Score: 31 points + opponent's deadwood

## Undercut
If knocker's deadwood equals or exceeds opponent's (after layoffs):
- Opponent scores 25 points + difference
- Knocker scores 0

## Game End
First player to 100 points wins. Winner gets:
- 100 point game bonus
- 25 points per hand won
- Line bonus if opponent won 0 hands

## Special Rules
- Aces are always low (A-2-3 valid, Q-K-A invalid)
- Cannot pick up discard if it was just discarded by opponent
- Deal alternates between hands
